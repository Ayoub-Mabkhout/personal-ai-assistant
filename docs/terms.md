# Terms of use

This project is open-source software for a self-hosted personal assistant. Each
installation is operated by one person, its owner, for their own private use. It is
not a public service, and no one else can sign up for or use an owner's installation.

- **Who may use it:** only the owner, with their own accounts and data.
- **Bank access:** account information is read through a licensed open-banking
  provider, only for accounts the owner has linked and consented to. The assistant
  does not initiate payments, and its insights are budgeting information, not
  financial or investment advice.
- **No warranty:** the software is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
  The owner is responsible for their own installation, credentials and data.
- **Third-party services:** use of the open-banking provider, the banks and any AI
  model provider is subject to their own terms.
